“How much is a yacht in Miami?” is the first question almost every guest asks us. Most answers online are a vague range or a teaser price that grows at checkout. This guide uses the listed rates of the 273 captained yachts on our site, so every number below is a real boat you can book.

The short answer

A captained 4-hour charter in Miami starts at $520 for a small day boat for up to 6 guests. For a group of 13 it starts at $605, for 25 guests at $1,440, and for 40 guests at $4,485. The typical 50 to 59 foot motor yacht, the most popular size for birthdays and bachelorettes, sits around $1,720 for 4 hours. Superyachts of 100 feet and up start at $5,750.

Listed rates include the boat, a licensed captain and crew. On top, plan for a 15 to 20% crew gratuity, plus anything you add, such as catering or decorations.

4, 6 or 8 hours?

Four hours is the standard minimum and enough for a sunset cruise or a sandbar afternoon, but longer charters are better value per hour: on our listings a 6-hour charter costs a median 31% more than the 4-hour rate, and 8 hours about 67% more. On the 60’ Aicon, for example, 4 hours is $1,495 and 6 hours is $1,955. If you want lunch at anchor and a sandbar, the longer block usually pays off.

Listed rates are weekend rates; if your dates are flexible, ask about weekdays.

What the listed rate leaves out

Before you pay a deposit, get each of these in writing:

  • Crew gratuity, 15–20%. Customary in Miami and almost never included. It is calculated on the charter rate and paid at the end of the trip.
  • Fuel. Normally included for a standard cruising route on the bay. Long runs, such as a trip up the coast, or fast performance boats can be billed extra.
  • Sales tax. Florida generally treats a crewed charter, where the operator’s captain runs the boat, as an exempt service. Some operators still add 7% (6% state plus the 1% Miami-Dade surtax). Ask which applies.
  • Marina fees. A few marinas charge boarding or parking fees. Confirm the dock and parking in advance.
  • Food, drinks and extras. Catering, decorations, a photographer and water toys are quoted separately. Many boats let you bring your own food and drinks.
  • Deposit and weather. Most operators take a deposit to hold the date. Ask what happens if a storm or small craft advisory cancels the trip.

A worked example

A birthday for 25 guests on the 60’ Aicon for 4 hours: $1,495 charter plus a 15–20% gratuity ($224–$299) comes to about $1,719–$1,794, or roughly $69–$72 per guest, before food and drinks. Every Latitude °25 quote sets out these lines before you confirm, so you can compare boats like for like.

Art Basel, Winterfest and New Year’s Eve

December is the busiest month on the water in South Florida, and three dates drive demand:

  • Art Basel Miami Beach, December 4–6, 2026, with VIP preview days on December 2 and 3 at the Miami Beach Convention Center. The City of Miami Beach runs Art Week from November 30 to December 6. Evening charters for client hosting go first. See our Art Basel yacht charter page for the yachts we recommend.
  • Winterfest Boat Parade, Saturday, December 12, 2026, from 6 PM in Fort Lauderdale. The parade runs about 12 miles from the New River up the Intracoastal to Lake Santa Barbara in Pompano Beach. Watching from the water means booking one of the few yachts based in Fort Lauderdale.
  • New Year’s Eve, Thursday, December 31. Fireworks go up over Biscayne Bay at midnight from the Bayfront Park celebration downtown, and Miami Beach usually holds its own beach fireworks. The Coast Guard sets safety zones around the fireworks, and your captain anchors outside them with a clear view. Read our New Year’s Eve yacht guide.

What changes on these dates is mostly the terms. Many operators set a minimum charter length for New Year’s Eve (often 5 to 6 hours) and a holiday rate above their standard listing. Settle your group size and budget now, shortlist two or three boats and request a written quote early; it confirms the rate for your exact date.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to rent a yacht in Miami for 4 hours?

Across the 273 yachts we list, 4-hour captained charters start at $520 for a small day boat for up to 6 guests, $605 for a group of 13, $1,440 for 25 guests and $4,485 for 40. Budget 15 to 20 percent on top for crew gratuity.

Is a 6 or 8-hour charter better value than 4 hours?

Usually, per hour. On our listings a 6-hour charter costs a median 31 percent more than the 4-hour rate and an 8-hour charter about 67 percent more, so you get double the time for well under double the price.

Do I need to tip the yacht crew in Miami?

Yes. A crew gratuity of 15 to 20 percent of the charter rate is customary in Miami and is almost never included in the listed price.

Do yacht prices go up during Art Basel and New Year's Eve?

Often. Listed rates are standard weekend rates. For peak dates such as Art Basel week (December 2 to 6, 2026) and New Year's Eve, many operators set minimum charter lengths and holiday rates, and the best evening slots book first. Your written quote confirms the exact rate for your date.

Is Florida sales tax added to a yacht charter?

It depends on how the charter is structured. Florida generally treats a charter where the operator supplies the captain and crew, and the guests do not direct the boat, as a service rather than a taxable rental. Some operators still add 7 percent (6 percent state plus the 1 percent Miami-Dade surtax). Your quote should state which applies.

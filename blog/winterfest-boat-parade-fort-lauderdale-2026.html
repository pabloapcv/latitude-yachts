How to Watch the 2026 Winterfest Boat Parade in Fort Lauderdale

The 55th Seminole Hard Rock Winterfest Boat Parade lights up 12 miles of the New River and the Intracoastal on Saturday, December 12. Here is how to watch it from shore or from a boat, plus a short Fort Lauderdale water bucket list if you are still in town.

The night, at a glance

In 2026 Winterfest falls on Saturday, December 12. A preshow of non-motorized vessels — paddleboards, kayaks and gondolas — starts at 5:30 p.m., and the parade starts at 6:00 p.m. sharp, rain or shine. Boats stage on the New River in downtown Fort Lauderdale, from west of the historic Stranahan House to the Broward Center for the Performing Arts, then run east to the Intracoastal and north to Lake Santa Barbara in Pompano Beach: a 12-mile parade. It takes about two and a half hours to pass any one spot, later the farther north you are, and is scheduled to reach the official Las Olas viewing area around 6:30 p.m. Times below come from the official 2026 Winterfest brochure and event pages; bridge openings are approximate.

How to watch from shore

You do not need a boat, or a ticket, to see the parade. Public stretches of the New River and the Intracoastal fill up early, including the banks around Las Olas, spots farther north, and the parks and seawalls in Pompano toward the finish. Get there well before 6 p.m., bring a chair, and plan to stay put.

If you want a reserved seat, food vendors, stage entertainment and a bathroom, the official public viewing area is at Las Olas Intracoastal Promenade Park, on the southeast corner of Las Olas Boulevard and the Intracoastal. For 2026:

  • Gates open at 4:00 p.m. The parade arrives around 6:30 p.m.
  • Tickets are $40 for adults, $30 for children 12 and under, and free for children 3 and under, sold as electronic tickets through Winterfest.
  • Seating is provided. The official rules say no bottles, coolers, chairs or pets.
  • Because the Las Olas bridge is locked open for the parade, you should be on the east side of the Las Olas bridge by 6:00 p.m. if that is your viewing area.

Parking at the Las Olas garage next to the park is limited. Winterfest encourages rideshare or Brightline. Confirm tickets and any shuttles on winterfestparade.com.

Watching from a boat

Watching from the water is the version people remember, but you have to be in position before the waterway closes to ordinary traffic, not after.

Winterfest’s 2026 waterway closings for non-parade vessels:

  • 2:30 p.m. — the New River closes from the Fork near Little Florida, west of Cooley’s Landing, east to Marker 9.
  • 4:50 p.m. — the Intracoastal closes from Port Everglades north to the Oakland Park bridge.
  • 5:50 p.m. — the Intracoastal closes from north of the Oakland Park bridge to Lake Santa Barbara.

Parade vessels line up on the New River between 3:00 and 4:30 p.m. No-anchor zones are enforced on the New River and at Birch State Park; Winterfest posts a spectator-anchoring map each year. The U.S. Coast Guard also sets a moving regulated area around the parade, and only designated parade and official patrol vessels may enter that path. Spectator boats stay clear, follow directions from patrol, and stay put until the parade has passed.

If you charter a boat for the night, book early, tell the captain you want to watch the parade, and choose a 6- or 8-hour block. A 4-hour charter that starts at 4 p.m. is already tight against the 4:50 p.m. Intracoastal closure.

A Fort Lauderdale water bucket list

If you are in town for more than parade night, these are the water things people usually tell you not to skip:

  • The Water Taxi, as a sightseeing loop, on a different day. Boats run daily, 10 a.m. to 10 p.m. An all-route day pass is $38 for adults and $18 for children ages 5 to 11; children under 5 ride free. An evening pass after 5 p.m. is $25 for adults. The main loop takes about three hours if you stay on board.
  • Snorkeling off the beach at Lauderdale-by-the-Sea. A shallow reef sits a short swim from the sand, just south of Anglin’s Pier. Go on a calm, flat morning, use a surface marker, and stay clear of the pier and fishing lines.
  • Sunset at the Hillsboro Inlet Lighthouse. Hillsboro Inlet Park in Pompano Beach looks across at the historic light, the drawbridge and the boats. Public tours of the lighthouse itself, when offered, are run by the Hillsboro Lighthouse Preservation Society.
  • A weekend afternoon at the Lake Boca sandbar. Boats raft up in waist-deep water near the Boca Inlet; you need to be on a boat to do it properly. See our Boca Raton yacht charters if you want to split a captained afternoon.

December is a busy month on the water. Art Basel Miami Beach runs December 4 to 6, 2026 (VIP days December 2 to 3), and New Year’s Eve follows two weeks after Winterfest. If you are booking a boat for any of those dates, start sooner than you think you need to. See our notes on Art Basel yacht charters and New Year’s Eve yachts.

Frequently asked questions

When is the Winterfest Boat Parade in 2026?

Saturday, December 12, 2026. A non-motorized preshow begins at 5:30 p.m., and the parade starts at 6:00 p.m. sharp. It takes about two and a half hours to pass any one spot.

Where is the best place to watch Winterfest from shore?

The official ticketed viewing area is at Las Olas Intracoastal Promenade Park. Gates open at 4 p.m. and the parade arrives around 6:30 p.m. Tickets are $40 for adults, $30 for children 12 and under, and free for children 3 and under. You can also watch for free from public spots along the route; arrive early and bring a chair.

Can I watch the Winterfest Boat Parade from a private boat?

Yes, if you are in position before the waterway closes and you stay out of the parade path. In 2026 the New River closes to non-parade boats at 2:30 p.m., the Intracoastal from Port Everglades to the Oakland Park bridge at 4:50 p.m., and north of Oakland Park to Lake Santa Barbara at 5:50 p.m. Book early, and plan a 6- or 8-hour charter.

Does the Fort Lauderdale Water Taxi run during the parade?

The Water Taxi is a good way to see the canals on any other day (all-route day passes are $38 for adults, 10 a.m. to 10 p.m.). On parade night the Intracoastal is closed to non-parade vessels, so do not count on riding it during the show. Confirm parade-day service directly with Water Taxi.

How much does it cost to charter a yacht to watch Winterfest?

It depends on the boat and the date.

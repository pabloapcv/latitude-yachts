South Florida’s Other Holiday Boat Parades, 2026: How to Watch from a Yacht

Winterfest gets the headlines, but it is one of six lighted boat parades on South Florida’s waterways this December. Two of them are in Miami. Here are the 2026 dates, routes and bridge times from each organizer, where a charter boat can actually watch from, and what an evening on the water costs, using real prices from our fleet.

Winterfest is the biggest, and it has its own rules, closures and bridge schedule, so we cover it separately in how to watch the 2026 Winterfest Boat Parade. This guide covers the other five. Two clashes to plan around: December 5 is also the Saturday of Art Basel Miami Beach, and the Pompano and Boynton–Delray parades run on the same Friday night, so you can only see one of them.

Holiday of Miracles Boat Parade, Biscayne Bay: Saturday, December 5

This parade is run by the Jackson Health Foundation as part of its toy drive for young patients at Holtz Children’s Hospital, and the lead boats carry patients and their families. Decorated boats sail through Miami Shores, Normandy Isles and North Bay Village, starting at sunset, and the foundation lists three public watch parties along the route. The City of Miami Beach’s free one is at Normandy Shores Park, 2401 Biarritz Drive, from 5 to 7 p.m. The city says the boats pass the north side of Miami Beach around 6:15 p.m., with fireworks at 6:30 p.m.

From the water. It happens in the north bay, away from downtown crowds, and boats that board in North Miami or Miami Beach are a short run away (see our Miami Beach yachts). Start late afternoon, watch the sunset, then let the captain pick a spot clear of the route.

Combine it with Art Week. A sunset-plus-parade charter is an easy evening off the fair shuttles; see our Art Basel yacht charter page if you are hosting clients. Bring a new, unwrapped toy for the drive.

Miami Outboard Club Holiday Boat Parade: Saturday, December 19

The Miami Outboard Club, on the MacArthur Causeway, hosts a yearly holiday parade of lighted boats across Biscayne Bay against the downtown skyline. For 2026 it is on Saturday, December 19. Bayfront Park, 301 Biscayne Boulevard, is an official free viewing site from 6 to 9 p.m., with DJs, family activities and food trucks. The club has not published a detailed route, so we don’t give one; expect the boats on the bay in front of downtown.

From the water. This one suits boats that board on the Miami River, in Brickell or at Bayside, minutes from the skyline. See Brickell and Downtown yachts. It suits a family outing during a visit from parents or a holiday office party: see corporate charters.

Pompano Beach and Boynton–Delray: Friday, December 11

Boca Raton no longer has its own parade; the city ended it in 2024. Instead, two parades run the same Friday night, one ending at each edge of Boca.

Pompano Beach (heading north). The Greater Pompano Beach Chamber of Commerce’s 64th Holiday Boat Parade starts at 6 p.m., with more than 50 boats expected and a theme of “A Party in the U.S.A.” for America’s 250th. The 2026 route had not been published when we checked. In 2025 the parade ran from Lake Santa Barbara north on the Intracoastal to Hillsboro Boulevard. The chamber also sells a $200 seat on its own parade boat and a $100 VIP viewing party at The Cove in Deerfield Beach.

Boynton Beach to Delray Beach (heading south). The City of Boynton Beach now leads this parade. Boats form near the Ocean Avenue Bridge in Lantana, head south at about 6:30 p.m., and finish near the C-15 Canal just south of Linton Boulevard in Delray Beach. The official watch site is Boynton Harbor Marina, 735 Casa Loma Boulevard. Approximate bridge openings: Ocean Avenue 7 to 7:45 p.m. and Woolbright Road 7:15 to 8 p.m. in Boynton, then George Bush Boulevard about 7:45, Atlantic Avenue about 8:00 and Linton Boulevard about 8:15 p.m. in Delray.

From the water. Both parades are within reach of Boca Raton’s Intracoastal, so a Boca charter can head north toward Delray or south toward Hillsboro, but not both in one evening. Our Boca Raton yachts include a 57’ and a 59’ Sunseeker for up to 25 guests, from $4,600 for 4 hours or $5,175 for 6. From Fort Lauderdale, see our Fort Lauderdale yachts: the 62’ Sunseeker takes up to 25 guests from $2,485 for 4 hours. Every bridge on the way is an opening, so the captain plans the run before you book.

Palm Beach: North Palm Beach to Jupiter, Saturday, December 5

The Marine Industries Association of Palm Beach County runs the 32nd Palm Beach Holiday Boat Parade, rain or shine. It leaves North Palm Beach at 6 p.m. behind a traveling fireworks display, heads north up the Intracoastal and reaches the Jupiter Inlet Lighthouse about 8 p.m. Estimated bridge openings: Parker around 6:00, PGA around 6:15, Donald Ross around 7:00, Indiantown Road around 7:30 and the Jupiter Federal Bridge around 7:45 p.m.

This is the one with the clearest rules for spectator boats. Under U.S. Coast Guard restrictions, spectators may not anchor, idle or drift in the parade channel. Boats that want to anchor must use the designated spectator anchoring area shown on the organizers’ map, which extends north into Jupiter. The organizers suggest Ski Beach or anywhere between Donald Ross Road and Indiantown Road that is out of the way. Law enforcement will move boats that anchor in front of North Palm Beach Marina or the North Palm Beach Country Club.

Jupiter is a long run up the Intracoastal from Boca, so ask us early and we will tell you honestly whether watching from shore makes more sense.

How a parade-night charter works

  • Book a 4-hour evening slot that starts before sunset. Sunset comes around 5:30 p.m. in early December. Start about an hour before; choose 6 hours for dinner on board.
  • You are a spectator, not a parade entry. Charter boats stay outside the parade channel, follow directions from patrol boats and anchor only where it is allowed.
  • Pick height and a cover. A flybridge gives the best view over other spectator boats. Bring a layer: December evenings on the water feel cool.
  • Group size sets the boat. Every boat has a firm guest limit. On our fleet that is up to 13 guests on most 33–40 foot boats, 18 on most 45–55 foot yachts, and 25 to 30 on larger yachts.
  • Book early for Saturdays. December 5 overlaps Art Basel Saturday and December 12 is Winterfest. Book two to three weeks ahead at minimum.

Frequently asked questions

When are the holiday boat parades in South Florida in 2026?

The Holiday of Miracles Boat Parade on Biscayne Bay and the Palm Beach Holiday Boat Parade (North Palm Beach to Jupiter) are both on Saturday, December 5. The Greater Pompano Beach Chamber parade and the Boynton Beach parade, which runs south to Delray Beach, are both on Friday, December 11. Winterfest is Saturday, December 12, and the Miami Outboard Club parade on Biscayne Bay is Saturday, December 19.

Is there a holiday boat parade in Miami?

Yes, two. The Jackson Health Foundation's Holiday of Miracles Boat Parade sails past Miami Shores, Normandy Isles and North Bay Village at sunset on Saturday, December 5, 2026. The Miami Outboard Club Holiday Boat Parade crosses Biscayne Bay in front of the downtown skyline on Saturday, December 19, 2026, with an official public viewing site at Bayfront Park.

Is there still a Boca Raton boat parade?

No. The City of Boca Raton ended its holiday boat parade in 2024. The nearest are the Boynton Beach parade, which ends in Delray Beach just south of Linton Boulevard, and the Pompano Beach parade. Both are on Friday, December 11, 2026.

Can a charter yacht watch a boat parade?

Yes, as a spectator. Charter boats must stay out of the parade channel, follow law enforcement directions and anchor only where it is allowed. Your captain chooses the spot on the night.

How much does it cost to watch a boat parade from a yacht?

Standard weekend rates on latitude25.us start at $720 for 4 hours for up to 13 guests on a 38-foot Sea Ray on the Miami River, $1,265 for up to 18 on a 48-foot Cruisers Yacht and $2,530 for up to 25 on a 60-foot Princess flybridge, with captain and crew included. A 15 to 20 percent crew gratuity is customary, and parade nights can carry holiday rates or longer minimums, so get a written quote for your date.

How early should I book a yacht for a holiday boat parade?

As early as you can. December 5 is also Art Basel Saturday and December 12 is Winterfest, so those evenings go first. Two to three weeks ahead is a sensible minimum.

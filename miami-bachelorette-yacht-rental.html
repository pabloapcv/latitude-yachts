Sandbar or skyline?

Haulover Sandbar is the lively one: music, other boats, shallow water to float in. Star Island, Fisher Island and the Biscayne Bay skyline are the calmer, more photogenic route, and a better fit if the bride wants pictures rather than a party. Many groups do both on a 6-hour charter.

Before you book any bachelorette boat

Ask whether the captain and crew, fuel and a floating mat are included, what the crew gratuity is (15 to 20% is customary in Miami), and the weather policy. Most operators also ask for no glass, no red wine and no confetti or glitter on board. Your concierge confirms all of it in writing with your quote.

Sample 6-hour bachelorette itinerary

A 6-hour block is the sweet spot for most groups: enough time for photos, a swim stop and a skyline cruise without rushing the bride.

  • 0:00–0:30 Board, safety brief, champagne toast on the bow.
  • 0:30–2:00 Cruise past Star Island and Fisher Island for photos with the Miami skyline.
  • 2:00–3:30 Anchor at a calm sandbar or Key Biscayne cove; floating mats out, playlist on.
  • 3:30–5:00 Optional Haulover sandbar energy, or a second photo loop along Government Cut.
  • 5:00–6:00 Sunset return toward your boarding marina; group photos on the flybridge.

Prefer a shorter sunset-only celebration? A 4-hour charter focused on the skyline still photographs beautifully. Planning a bigger day? See our Miami yacht rental hub for size and route options, or board closer to Haulover from Aventura and North Miami.

Where bachelorette groups usually board

Most Miami bachelorette charters board from Miami Beach (South Beach and Sunset Harbour areas) or Downtown / Brickell along the Miami River and Biscayne Bay. Groups staying in Sunny Isles, Aventura or Bal Harbour often prefer a North Miami / Aventura boarding for a quicker run to Haulover Sandbar. Your concierge confirms the marina with the vessel — never assume a specific dock until the quote says so.

  • Miami Beach yacht charters — largest fleet, skyline and sandbar access.
  • Brickell & Downtown — convenient if the hotel is on the mainland.
  • Aventura & North Miami — closest to Haulover for sandbar-first days.

Bachelorette packing checklist

  • Soft-sided cooler and drinks — no glass on most boats; cans and plastic only.
  • Reef-safe sunscreen, hats, cover-ups and a light layer for the ride home.
  • Bluetooth playlist offline in case cell service dips on the bay.
  • Decor that leaves no glitter — balloons and banners are usually fine; confetti is not.
  • Cash or Venmo for crew gratuity (15–20% of the charter rate is customary).
  • IDs for anyone drinking and a small dry bag for phones.

Frequently asked

How much does a bachelorette yacht cost in Miami?

Listed 4-hour rates in our fleet start at $605 for up to 13 guests, $805 for up to 18 and $1,440 for up to 25. Weekends in peak season and add-ons such as catering or decor cost more; gratuity is usually separate.

How many people fit on a party yacht?

It depends on the yacht’s license: most sport yachts take up to 13 guests, 44 to 55-foot motor yachts up to 18, and 57 to 70-foot yachts up to 25.

Can we bring our own drinks and decorations?

Usually yes for drinks and simple decor. Most operators do not allow glass, red wine, confetti or glitter. Your concierge confirms each yacht’s rules.

Is a captain included?

Yes. Our charters are captained by licensed crews, so nobody in your group needs boating experience.

What happens if it rains?

Each operator has its own weather policy. Summer afternoon storms are common in Miami, so morning departures are safer from June to September. Your concierge explains the policy before you book.

How far in advance should we book?

For a Saturday in peak season (November through April), request 2–4 weeks ahead if you want a shortlist of sizes. Weekdays and summer dates are often available with a few days’ notice.

Can we board from Aventura or Sunny Isles?

Yes. Several vessels board in the North Miami / Aventura area, which shortens the run to Haulover Sandbar. See our Aventura yacht charter page.

Is food included?

Usually not. Most groups bring their own snacks and drinks or add catering through the concierge. Confirm the yacht’s galley and cooler space when you shortlist.

Do we need a boating license?

No. Every charter we arrange is captained by a licensed crew.

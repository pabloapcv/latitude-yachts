Guest limits are set by the yacht’s license and include everyone on board except crew. If your group is on the border, size up: a bow lounge and shade matter more than you think by hour three.

Sandbar or skyline?

Haulover Sandbar is the lively one: music, other boats, shallow water to float in. Star Island, Fisher Island and the Biscayne Bay skyline are the calmer, more photogenic route, and a better fit if the bride wants pictures rather than a party. Many groups do both on a 6-hour charter.

Before you book any bachelorette boat

Ask whether the captain and crew, fuel and a floating mat are included, what the crew gratuity is (15 to 20% is customary in Miami), and the weather policy. Most operators also ask for no glass, no red wine and no confetti or glitter on board. Your concierge confirms all of it in writing with your quote.

Frequently asked

How much does a bachelorette yacht cost in Miami?

Listed 4-hour rates in our fleet start at $605 for up to 13 guests, $805 for up to 18 and $1,440 for up to 25. Weekends in peak season and add-ons such as catering or decor cost more; gratuity is usually separate.

How many people fit on a party yacht?

It depends on the yacht’s license: most sport yachts take up to 13 guests, 44 to 55-foot motor yachts up to 18, and 57 to 70-foot yachts up to 25.

Can we bring our own drinks and decorations?

Usually yes for drinks and simple decor. Most operators do not allow glass, red wine, confetti or glitter. Your concierge confirms each yacht’s rules.

Is a captain included?

Yes. Our charters are captained by licensed crews, so nobody in your group needs boating experience.

What happens if it rains?

Each operator has its own weather policy. Summer afternoon storms are common in Miami, so morning departures are safer from June to September. Your concierge explains the policy before you book.

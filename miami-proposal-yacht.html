How a yacht proposal comes together

  • Time it to sunset. In Miami the sun sets around 5:30 PM in winter and after 8 PM in summer. Board about 90 minutes before sunset so the moment lands at golden hour.
  • Tell the captain the plan. The crew can anchor facing west toward the skyline, keep the ring hidden in the cabin and give you privacy on the bow at the right moment.
  • Add what matters to you. Flowers, champagne, a dinner on board, or a photographer hidden on the upper deck. Each is quoted separately so you see the full cost.
  • Have a weather plan. If a storm rolls in, most operators will move you to another time or date; your concierge confirms the policy in advance.

Two people or the whole family?

For just the two of you, a 25 to 43-foot boat is intimate and easy to arrange. If family and friends are waiting to celebrate after the yes, a 55 to 65-foot motor yacht gives you a private bow for the moment and a salon for the party.

Frequently asked

How much does a proposal yacht cost in Miami?

Listed 4-hour rates in our fleet start at $520 for a small day boat, around $2,000 for a designer 40 to 43-foot yacht and from about $1,500 for a 55 to 60-foot motor yacht. Flowers, catering and a photographer are added on request.

What is the best time for a proposal cruise?

Sunset. Board about 90 minutes before sunset, which is around 5:30 PM in winter and after 8 PM in summer, so you are anchored with the skyline in view at golden hour.

Can the crew help keep it a surprise?

Yes. Tell your concierge the plan; the captain can hide the ring, set up the bow and give you privacy at the right moment.

Can you arrange a photographer?

Yes, a photographer can usually be arranged on board and quoted separately.

Can we have dinner on the yacht?

On most yachts over 45 feet, yes. A private chef or catered dinner can be arranged and is quoted with your charter.

Planning to pop the question?

Tell your concierge the date and the moment you are imagining. We keep it discreet, and we will make sure the timing is right.

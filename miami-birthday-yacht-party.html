Plan it in four decisions

  • Group size. Up to 6 guests from $520 for 4 hours, up to 13 from $605, up to 18 from $805 and up to 25 from $1,440 (listed rates).
  • Daytime or sunset. Daytime for the sandbar and swimming; sunset for dinner, drinks and the skyline lit up.
  • Length. Four hours suits most parties; six hours lets you add a sandbar stop and a relaxed lunch.
  • Extras. Cake, balloons and decor (no confetti), catering, a DJ or a photographer, each quoted separately.

Milestone birthday?

For 30th, 40th and 50th celebrations with 25 to 40 guests, a 70 to 100-foot yacht with a full crew gives you a proper party deck, a salon and catering service. Tell your concierge it is a milestone and we will shortlist accordingly.

Frequently asked

How much does a birthday yacht party cost in Miami?

Listed 4-hour rates in our fleet start at $520 for up to 6 guests, $605 for up to 13, $805 for up to 18 and $1,440 for up to 25. Catering, decor and gratuity are extra.

Can we bring a birthday cake?

Yes. Bring your own cake or ask your concierge to arrange one. Most operators ask that decorations avoid confetti and glitter.

Is food included?

Not usually. You can bring your own food on most yachts or add catering, quoted with your charter.

Do we need a boating license?

No. Every charter we arrange comes with a licensed captain and crew.

How far in advance should we book?

A few days is often enough on weekdays. For weekends from November to April, book one to two weeks ahead.

About

South Florida's fleet, in one place

Latitude °25 — named for Miami's own line of latitude — exists because finding a charter yacht in South Florida usually means scrolling through a dozen Instagram pages, texting three different captains, and hoping someone calls back. We pulled the fleet into one searchable place.

We are not a yacht owner or a booking agent for any single operator. We're a concierge layer: you tell us what you're looking for, we match you to the right vessel, and we handle every conversation with the operator on your behalf — so you never have to chase down contact information or negotiate directly.

Good to know

How is pricing determined?

Listed prices are operator weekend rates for 4, 6, and 8-hour charters. Your concierge confirms the final rate — including any weekday, holiday, fuel, or gratuity adjustments — before you commit.

Can I pay online?

Not yet. Requests are reviewed by our concierge team, who arrange payment directly with you once your charter is confirmed.

What if my date isn't available?

Your concierge will offer the closest available dates or a comparable vessel in the same area and size class.

Do you charge a booking fee?

No request or browsing fees. Pricing shown is the operator's rate.

How many guests can a vessel hold?

Guest counts shown are an editorial estimate based on vessel length, not an official USCG passenger certificate. Your concierge confirms exact capacity when booking.

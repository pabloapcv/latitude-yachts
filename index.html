OUR CONCIERGE

One team. Every detail.

A concierge referral service connecting you with South Florida’s independent charter operators.

Latitude °25 — named for Miami’s own line of latitude — exists because finding a charter yacht in South Florida usually means scrolling through a dozen Instagram pages, texting three different captains, and hoping someone calls back. We pulled the fleet into one searchable place.

We are not a yacht owner or a booking agent for any single operator. We’re a concierge layer: you tell us what you’re looking for, we match you to the right vessel, and we handle every conversation with the operator on your behalf — so you never have to chase down contact information or negotiate directly.

What we do

You deal with one concierge team from first inquiry to the dock — never a patchwork of operators and phone numbers. Every listing is checked against real pricing and specs before it goes live — no stale posts or guesswork. Weekend rates for 4, 6, and 8-hour charters are shown up front, so you know the range before you ever reach out.

Where we operate

Boarding locations across greater South Florida: Miami Beach, Downtown Miami & Brickell, Key Biscayne, Fort Lauderdale, Coconut Grove, North Miami, and Boca Raton.

The Site lets you browse available vessels and submit a request to book. Submitting a request is an inquiry, not a confirmed reservation. Privacy Policy

Last updated October 31, 2026.

Terms of Service Privacy Policy

1. Information we collect

When you submit a booking request or contact form, we collect the information you provide — name, email, phone number, and your message or charter details (preferred date, party size, vessel of interest). We don't collect payment information through the Site, because the Site doesn't process payments.

2. Information stored in your browser

Favorites and past requests are stored locally in your browser (via localStorage) so they persist between visits on that device. This data stays on your device — it is not transmitted to us or visible to anyone else unless you submit a request through a form.

3. How we use your information

We use the information you submit to respond to your inquiry, check availability with the relevant Operator, and coordinate your charter. We do not sell your information or use it for third-party advertising.

4. Who we share it with

We share only what's necessary to fulfill your request — typically your name, contact details, and charter details — with the specific vessel Operator you've inquired about, so they can confirm availability and pricing.

5. Data retention

We retain inquiry information for as long as reasonably needed to handle your request and for basic business recordkeeping. You can ask us to delete your information at any time (see below).

6. Your choices

You can clear your locally stored favorites and request history at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this domain. To request deletion of information you've submitted to us, email concierge@latitude25.us.

7. Children's privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time; the "last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

Why charter out of Downtown Miami & Brickell

If you’re staying or working in Brickell or Downtown, this is the boarding area that saves you the drive. Marinas along the Miami River and Biscayne Bay put you on the water in minutes, with a clear run out past the Downtown skyline to open bay — no detour to South Beach or Key Biscayne required. It’s also the area with the sharpest mix of smaller, faster boats: more sport yachts and day boats per listing than anywhere else in the fleet, ideal for a half-day on the water without committing to a full-size yacht.

Boarding locations

Good to know before you book

Rates shown on each vessel’s listing are for 4, 6, and 8-hour charters and typically include captain and crew; fuel, gratuity, and catering are usually separate and confirmed with your quote. Because the fleet here skews smaller and faster, it’s a strong option if you want to get on the water the same week.

Frequently asked

How close are the marinas to Brickell and Downtown?

Most boarding locations are a short rideshare from Brickell or Downtown — typically under 10 minutes. Your concierge sends the exact marina address once your vessel is confirmed.

Is this a good option for a short, same-week booking?

Yes — the smaller, faster boats based here tend to have more same-week availability than the larger Miami Beach fleet. Ask your concierge for the fastest option if your date is close.

Can I combine a Brickell charter with a trip out to the sandbars?

Depends on the vessel’s range and your charter length — for a shorter 4-hour charter, most captains stay closer to the bay and Downtown skyline; a 6 or 8-hour charter can usually reach the sandbars near Key Biscayne. Ask your concierge when requesting.

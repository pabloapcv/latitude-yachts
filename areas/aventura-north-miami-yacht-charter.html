Why charter in Aventura & North Miami

Hotels and condos line the coast from Bal Harbour through Sunny Isles and Aventura, but the water is often less crowded than central South Beach. A half-day charter can cruise the northern Intracoastal, pass the high-rises of Sunny Isles, and still reach the Haulover inlet for Atlantic views or a sandbar stop when conditions allow.

That geography matters if your group is staying in north Miami-Dade: boarding near North Miami Beach or the Haulover waterfront saves drive time compared with trekking to Miami Beach marinas. For birthdays, proposals, or a relaxed family day, this corridor delivers open water without the South Beach bustle.

Neighborhoods we serve

  • Aventura — ideal for resort and mall-district guests who want a yacht day without crossing the causeways downtown.
  • Sunny Isles Beach — long beachfront skyline and easy runs north toward Haulover or south along the coast.
  • North Miami Beach — practical boarding for groups based between the beach and the mainland.
  • Bal Harbour — luxury-hotel guests and shoppers who want a polished on-water experience close to home.
  • Haulover — closest access to the famous sandbar and the Atlantic for swimming and sunset cruises.

Boarding & meeting your yacht

Each yacht boards from a marina serving one of the neighborhoods above—your concierge confirms the boarding area by neighborhood name, parking guidance, and arrival time once your vessel is locked in. If your hotel is in Aventura or Sunny Isles, ask about boats that minimize travel to the dock.

Frequently asked

How much does a yacht charter in Aventura or North Miami cost?

North Miami and Aventura fleet listings start around $520 for a 4-hour charter on a 21-foot Sea Ray for up to 6 guests, and run up to about $6,095 for a 4-hour charter on an 86-foot Azimut with jacuzzi and jet ski for up to 30 guests. Your concierge confirms the exact rate before you book.

Where do North Miami and Aventura charters board?

Boarding depends on the yacht you choose. Most vessels in this area leave from waterfront marinas serving Aventura, North Miami Beach, Sunny Isles Beach, Bal Harbour, or the Haulover area. Your concierge sends neighborhood-level boarding details and timing when your yacht is confirmed.

Can we anchor at the Haulover sandbar?

Yes, when weather and tide allow. Yachts boarding in north Miami-Dade are often closer to Haulover than South Beach fleets, which means a shorter run to the sandbar for swimming and lounging. Your captain chooses a safe spot based on conditions and local rules.

Is a captain included?

Yes. Every charter Latitude25 arranges is operated by a licensed captain and crew. You do not need a boating license to enjoy the day on the water.

How far in advance should we book?

Weekends, holidays, and peak winter season fill quickly—especially larger yachts. Reaching out a week or more ahead helps secure your preferred vessel; last-minute requests are possible when boats are available. Your concierge confirms availability before you commit.

North Miami vs Miami Beach: which is better for a charter?

North Miami and Aventura charters suit guests staying in Sunny Isles, Aventura, or Bal Harbour who want a quieter cruise, quick access to Haulover, and less congested waterways than central South Beach. For the largest fleet and Star Island skyline runs, see our Miami Beach charters; many north Dade guests still choose boats here for convenience.

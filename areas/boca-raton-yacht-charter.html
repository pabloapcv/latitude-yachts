Why charter in Boca Raton

Lake Boca Raton, just inside the Boca Inlet, is where locals anchor on weekends to swim and socialize. Beyond it, the Intracoastal Waterway runs past some of South Florida’s most beautiful waterfront homes, and the inlet opens onto clear Atlantic water for a coastal cruise.

Who it suits

Families visiting Boca, milestone birthdays, client hosting from the Boca and Palm Beach business community, and anyone who wants a calmer alternative to Miami’s busiest spots.

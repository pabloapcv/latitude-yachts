Why charter in Fort Lauderdale

The “Venice of America” has more navigable waterways than almost anywhere in Florida. A typical half-day charter cruises the Intracoastal Waterway past the mansions of Las Olas Isles and Harbor Beach, loops up the New River through downtown, and can anchor in Lake Sylvia for a swim on a calm day.

Winterfest Boat Parade, December 12, 2026

The Winterfest Boat Parade lights up the New River and Intracoastal each December. Watching from the water means planning ahead: viewing boats book early and must stay clear of the parade route, so your captain picks a safe vantage point.

Miami or Fort Lauderdale?

Fort Lauderdale is calmer and more residential, ideal for a relaxed cruise or a family celebration. If you want the Miami skyline, sandbars or the largest choice of yachts, see our Miami Beach charters; many groups staying in Fort Lauderdale are happy to drive 40 minutes south.

Frequently asked

How much does a yacht charter in Fort Lauderdale cost?

Our Fort Lauderdale yachts are listed from $2,485 for 4 hours on a 62-foot Sunseeker for up to 25 guests, and from $5,405 for a 78-foot Ferretti for up to 30 guests.

Where do Fort Lauderdale charters board?

Fort Lauderdale yachts board from marinas along the Intracoastal and New River; your concierge confirms the marina when the yacht is booked.

Can we watch the Winterfest Boat Parade from a yacht?

Yes, with early planning. The parade is on December 12, 2026; viewing yachts book early and stay clear of the route.

Is a captain included?

Yes. Every charter we arrange is captained by a licensed crew.
